## File inspection

The inherited PNGs and a rendered thumbnail of the Bitcoin SVG were visually inspected. PNG chunks were enumerated for text, compressed text, international text/XMP, EXIF, and timestamps; none of those chunks were present. Remaining chunks contain image pixels, palettes, transparency, and optional standard color/gamma information. No identity from the local computer was added. Necessary third-party provenance is preserved above.

## Playground artwork

The sky-blue and pink environment in assets/playground-background.png and the transparent cabinet in assets/solpot-machine-display.png were created using the built-in image-generation tool from the selected Playground concept. The upper marquee is an empty gold-framed display for live jackpot numbers. The reels, lever, buttons and balances are live HTML/CSS. No artist attribution is claimed.
